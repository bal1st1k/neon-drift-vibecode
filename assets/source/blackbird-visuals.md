## Studio hero
Generated with built-in imagegen using blackbird-sprite.jpg as reference; PNG retained in for promt/blackbird/blackbird-hero.png and WebP delivery copy blackbird-hero.webp. Imagegen default output was copied into this workspace.

Prompt:
Use case: product-mockup. Create a premium photorealistic studio hero photograph of the classic black Porsche 911 Turbo 3.3 Typ 930 represented in the reference top-down sprite. This is a NEW camera view, not a top-down image. Show a full car in low front-left three-quarter view, nose pointing left, tail to right. Period correct late-1970s/1980s 930 coupe, round raised headlights, black Fuchs wheels with polished rims, black impact bumpers, wide rear arches, large tea-tray turbo wing. Glossy deep black body. Bright seamless off-white studio background and floor, soft natural contact shadow. Extremely clean large side-profile silhouette read, camera at headlight height with long lens, plenty of breathing room around car; vehicle fills about 75 percent of landscape width, whole car and both ends visible. No text, people, watermark, cinematic smoke or modern 911 details. Editorial automotive product photography, restrained elegant lighting. Wide landscape format.

Historical photo sources and design decisions: docs/blackbird/DESIGN.md. Gallery Aaldering image requests returned 403 and no assets from that site were used.
